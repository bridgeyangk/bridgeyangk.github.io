Short Bio

I am currently a PhD student at Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen) (HITSZ), supervised by Prof. Xun Zhou. My research focuses on trustworthy AI and spatial-temporal recommendation systems.

Before that, I received my B.Eng. and M.Eng. degrees from the School of Information and Software Engineering, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC) in 2022 and 2025, respectively.

Selected Publications

ExNext: Self-Explainable Next POI Recommendation [Paper] [Code]
Kai Yang, Yi Yang, Qiang Gao, Ting Zhong, Yong Wang, Fan Zhou
SIGIR 2024, CCF-A

We introduce ExNext, a novel framework for POI recommendation that improves trustworthiness by balancing accuracy and explainability, leveraging information theory to learn compact, informative representations.

ExGeo: Exploring Self-Explainable Street-Level IP Geolocation with Graph Information Bottleneck [Paper] [Code]
Kai Yang, Wenxin Tai, Zhenhui Li, Ting Zhong, Guangqiang Yin, Yong Wang, Fan Zhou
ICASSP 2024, CCF-B

We endow the IP geolocation model with explainability via variational graph information bottleneck, balancing concise explanation and informative representation.

TCGeo: Improving IP Geolocation With Target-Centric IP Graph (Student Abstract) [Paper]
Kai Yang, Jiayang Li, Wenxin Tai, Zhenhui Li, Ting Zhong, Guangqiang Yin, Yong Wang, Fan Zhou
AAAI 2024, CCF-A

We propose a target-centric IP graph to mitigate sparsity, enhancing contextual information utilization in network topology for geolocation.

LSGAT: Interpreting Temporal Knowledge Graph Reasoning (Student Abstract) [Paper]
Bin Chen, Kai Yang, Wenxin Tai, Zhangtao Cheng, Leyuan Liu, Ting Zhong, Fan Zhou
AAAI 2024, CCF-A

An interpretable temporal knowledge graph reasoning method with competitive prediction accuracy, which identifies pivotal historical events for explainability.
